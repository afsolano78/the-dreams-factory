The answer is simple: no. Choosing a place to get married in Asturias today is not what it used to be.

In a civil ceremony you have the chance to do, say and hear everything you had imagined for your wedding day. There is no more open ceremony than a civil one, whether it’s led by a councillor, a mayor, a celebrant or, of course, a friend.

You can choose the place you like best to hold it. A few years ago this was unthinkable in Spain: we only saw it in American films. Now it’s up to you: by the sea, in a forest, in a cabin or in a garden. You choose the place and we turn it into a fairy-tale setting for the big moment.

The legal articles read during the ceremony can feel very dry and not exactly romantic, so spacing them out with music at the special moments makes it all much warmer. A live band, a choir, jazz, gospel, opera or even the original versions of the songs that have been part of your relationship are some of the options. Anything goes: it’s your moment!

Ask someone special to say a few words. There’s no doubt they’ll speak from the heart and move you. That’s how you make the ceremony yours and yours alone.

Still think civil ceremonies are cold? That’s because you haven’t had yours yet.
