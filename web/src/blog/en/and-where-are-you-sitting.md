We admit it: seating plans add a touch of originality to a wedding’s décor, and we’re big fans of them for several reasons.

They are usually one of the guests’ first contacts with the celebration, so they set one of the first impressions. They also liven up the wait and give guests a fun moment while they look for where they’ll be sitting. And they are a great decorative element that goes with the rest of the party, as they almost always match its style.

But careful! As we said, we’re fans, as long as a few important things are kept in mind.

Very often so much attention goes into how original or decorative they are that their real job is forgotten: telling guests where to sit. They need to be practical; after all, the idea is to make things easy for guests, not to turn that moment into reading a map or solving a puzzle. Our experience tells us there will always be someone who struggles to understand it. That’s why we always recommend having someone from our team coordinating that moment, to help guests find their name on the seating plan and then their table when they walk into the reception.

If you take care of these things, seating plans are a place to let your imagination run wild, give the celebration a distinctive touch and surprise everyone.

So, an original seating plan at your wedding? Absolutely.
