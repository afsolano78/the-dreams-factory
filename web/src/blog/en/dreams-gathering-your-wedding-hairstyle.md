As wedding planners, we have always liked our couples to talk directly to suppliers who specialise in weddings. These are very rewarding conversations, where everything flows and you end up talking not only about that supplier’s field but about much more. They are a great help for couples: they leave with a much fuller picture of what the wedding day involves, and with a lot more peace of mind.

This time we wanted brides-to-be to meet in our space and discover first-hand the latest trends in hairstyles and headpieces, with advice on different looks, hairstyles to suit each hair texture and colour, treatments… As you know, everything to do with planning a wedding always has more to it than meets the eye, and being well informed is essential to make the right choice in the end.

Cari, from **La Mona Riza**, a bridal specialist, told us everything you need to know for the wedding day. Meanwhile, the brides could see, touch and try on the beautiful headpieces by **Los Ojos Chiribitas**: Lucía makes them to measure from scratch, and they suit any hairstyle and personality.

After the talk we carried on sharing ideas and discovering the wonderful plans our guests had for their weddings, this time over coffee and the sweet treats **Porto Creaciones** had prepared for us. Their dessert tables are no ordinary tables: they are real desserts in miniature that nobody can resist. Our guests can vouch for it, and they also took home a few little gifts.

We can show you how it went thanks to the photos by **M2 Visual Studio**, whose lens didn’t miss a single detail.

A great plan for a Sunday morning, and one we will repeat soon! Thank you so much to everyone who came.
