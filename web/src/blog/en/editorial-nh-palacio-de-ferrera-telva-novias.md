Avilés is our town, and nothing makes us happier than working there. So when **NH Collection Palacio de Avilés** asked us to take part in this project as wedding planners, we didn’t hesitate.

Together with a great team of professionals, we created this editorial in one of the gems hidden in Avilés: a hotel steeped in history, with elegance, luxury and comfort. The surprise comes when you walk in and discover that its main room is a conservatory that blends with the nature of the town park…

The photography was by **JFK Imagen Social**. We were joined by Ana Gayoso, creator of *La Champanera*, who was attending her best friend’s wedding dressed by **Marcos Luengo**, who also dressed the bride in a wonderful gown with a cape. Make-up for both was by **Lorena Carbajal**, and Ana, from **Las Horquillas de Anita**, did their hair. The looks were completed with shoes by Miss García and jewellery by Graciela Sampedro.

We took care of designing the spaces and coordinating the shoot. After visiting the palace, we prepared the sketches we later presented to **Leymar Floristas**, who know the venue inside out and captured exactly what we asked for: the bouquet, the flowers for the main room and for the rest of the spaces.

We chose a very natural décor, with a palette of rose quartz, burgundy and green that made one of the palace’s wrought-iron doors stand out, dressed in a loose arrangement of ivy, eucalyptus, flowers and candles. The bouquet followed the same tones: wild, natural and unstructured.

The shoot was featured in **Telva Novias**, in the story “Casarse en un palacio asturiano” (Getting married in an Asturian palace). [You can see this and other editorials on our weddings page](/en/weddings/).
