The aim of our blog has always been to give you information that helps if you’re in the middle of planning a wedding. Whether it’s inspiration or more practical information, our goal as wedding planners is to lend you a hand.

Today the credit isn’t ours. There are lovely, very professional people who have the great idea of sharing their work, and we discovered it on social media. And what work it is!

It’s a fantastic wedding planner created by May, from **Creative Mindly**, and Margarita, from **Yo Diré Que Sí**, authors of brilliant blogs about DIY, graphic design and lots of curiosities.

The planner is full of detail and every important piece of information has its own space —and we should know, given the amount of organisation and control a wedding requires: suppliers, dates, budgets, lists… All that whirlwind of information has its place in this planner, in an efficient and, let’s be honest, beautiful way. After all, it’s your wedding!

Here are the links with all the information to download it (in Spanish):

- [The planner on Creative Mindly](http://www.creativemindly.com/2015/11/agenda-bonita-gratis-para-organizar-boda.html)
- [The planner on Yo Diré Que Sí](http://yodirequesi.blogspot.com/2015/11/agenda-para-bodas-molonas-descargala.html)

Once again, thank you both for sharing work made with so much love.

*Note: the planner was designed for 2016 to 2018, but its supplier, budget and list templates are just as useful today. If you’d rather we helped you put everything in order, [an online consultation with us](/en/online-consultations/) can be a good place to start.*
