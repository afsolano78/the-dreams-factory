As wedding planners, we always keep up with the trends in the wedding world, especially in Asturias.

One of the parts we enjoy most is the look of the wedding: getting to know the couple’s tastes and watching an idea take shape, showing them different options, adding their essence and creating something magical they identify with one hundred per cent.

Although there are many more important things at a wedding than how it looks, it is the most visible part, the one guests remember, so surprising them with a special setting is part of our job. That’s why we like to keep up with everything happening in the industry, and we had been wanting to see a hand-lettering workshop first-hand for a while.

Hand lettering is a technique for writing by hand that can be used to create all kinds of stationery, signs, posters and a thousand other things for an event.

Keen to find out how much there was to this technique (and believe me, there’s a lot), we didn’t hesitate when Vintage Bodas invited us to a workshop run by **Naranjas Chinas**. Aída knows an incredible amount about letters: their history, techniques, markers, brushes, nibs, styles of calligraphy (traditional, modern…), and she teaches with infinite patience. Naturally, ours was the beginners’ workshop: we loved everything we learned and were left wanting more.

Sharing a morning with colleagues from the industry is always great too: Dreams of Princess, Elena from Más que Eventos, Elena from Flores El Invernadero (a star pupil, she was so good at it!), Adri from En Tonos Pastel, Ana, Laura, María…

We encourage you to try it if you get the chance: you’ll find it isn’t as easy as it looks, and you’ll come home wanting to fill your walls with beautiful letters!
