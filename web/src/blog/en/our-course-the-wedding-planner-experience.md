> “There is no greater competition in our profession than a bad wedding planner. We believe there is a lack of training and information about what our work really is.”

That was the motto we opened our course **The Wedding Planner Experience** with, and it has been our main motivation for training future wedding planners.

For some years we had been toying with the idea of creating a course with real, useful content, where participants could learn everything about this profession and where we could show them what truly matters: planning the wedding, choosing and working with suppliers, coordinating the big day and, of course, the look of the wedding.

We designed a mainly theoretical programme, joined over the weekend by colleagues from the industry: M2 Visual Studio, Perspective, Liebling Maestro de Ceremonias, Siapro, Leymar Floristas, As de Bodas and A de Lola were with us. The students saw how a wedding planner works hand in hand with every professional at an event, and how much those professionals value that help.

We wanted to create a special atmosphere and get away from the classroom format. The Palacio de Avilés welcomed us and gave us exactly the atmosphere we were looking for. One of its wonderful rooms became our home for those three days, with its amazing coffee breaks between talks, a tour of the whole building, lunch in the kitchens while we watched the hotel team at work, and a final brunch in the palace’s private room.

The surprise was meeting such incredible students, ready to soak up every piece of information like sponges and passionate about everything we told them. Their words after the course have only made us want to get started on the next one:

> “Thank you for the transparency and warmth you showed from the very beginning when sharing the details of your working tools, and for explaining everything so simply and clearly.” *Lidia*

> “Thank you from the bottom of my heart for all the care you put into planning and running the course, because I promise it shows.” *Lucía*

> “My expectations were very high because I’m very demanding, and I honestly can’t find a single fault.” *María*

All this, and a little more, can be seen in the photos by M2 Visual Studio: a reflection of the success of this first edition of The Wedding Planner Experience.

*Vanessa and Beatriz*
