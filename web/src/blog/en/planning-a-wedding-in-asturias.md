More and more people who aren’t from Asturias write to us saying how beautiful this part of northern Spain is and that they want to get married here. Sometimes neither of them is from here, but they happily tell us why they decided to celebrate their wedding in Asturias, while we nod proudly… They say we don’t value what’s close to home, and it’s often true, but the reasons to get married in Asturias are endless: all kinds of landscapes, sea and mountains, wonderful towns, delicious food and the best suppliers, ready to make your wedding the party you’ve always imagined.

That said, planning a wedding in Asturias isn’t easy if you want the result to be perfect. You need someone who knows the area well and can bring to life all the ideas couples have in their heads, something that gets much harder when you don’t live here. That’s where we come in as wedding planners and take the reins of planning your wedding.

Then come a series of conversations —most of them remote— in which you tell us your story, your tastes and hobbies, you tell us about your families, and together we start building your big day. That way, without being here, you build your wedding as if the miles didn’t exist, in your own personal paradise: Asturias.

Often the couple aren’t present at any point during the planning, and we don’t meet them in person until a few days before. We have been their eyes and hands for months: we’ve been to meetings with every supplier, booked services, tasted menus, listened to musicians… But at the same time we’ve exchanged so many emails and calls that, when they see the result, everything is exactly as they wanted. Seeing the couple and their guests happy and amazed by what’s happening at every moment is the best proof that we’ve done our job well.

Of course, a wedding planner isn’t only for couples who don’t live where they’re getting married. More and more couples realise that working with us makes planning their wedding much more relaxed: they know we’ll find the professionals who best match their tastes, save them time and doubts about the unknown, advise them on whatever they need, and that they’ll enjoy both the run-up and the day itself, because everything will flow.

A wedding planner makes a difference in many more ways than you imagine, and you only realise it when you start thinking about your big day. At The Dreams Factory we’d love to help you and turn it into a real experience.
