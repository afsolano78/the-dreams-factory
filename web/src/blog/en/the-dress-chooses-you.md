Yes, ladies (because this post is for you): it’s that time of year for choosing the dress for the big day. The last few months of the year are when bridal shops, ateliers and workshops are busiest, with wardrobes bursting with new collections. Although they are presented every May, it isn’t usually until October that you can see them complete in store.

And this is when brides live one of the most special and exciting moments of the whole rollercoaster that is a wedding.

Looking for your dress shouldn’t become a chore. Enjoy it and share it with people who are just as excited about it as you are. But careful with the “people” part! If you take too many, you risk getting overwhelmed by so many opinions, especially if you don’t have a clear idea.

Even if you know exactly what you want, don’t refuse to try other options: you’ll be surprised. You surely know brides who ended up choosing something completely different from what they had in mind. Hence the title of this post: most of the time it’s the dress that chooses you, because of that detail at the neckline, that full hem that looks so good or that cut that defines the waist… Every dress holds a surprise.

Make sure you feel comfortable in the dress you choose. Move around, twirl, raise your arms… If the dress is well made and fitted to your body, it needn’t be uncomfortable, however voluminous it is.

Don’t rush! Don’t decide until you’re really sure. We’ve all seen that moment on TV when a bride tries on a dress and exclaims: “This is it, this is my dress!”. Well, in real life that happens in only a few cases, so you need to be sure. There are many months to go until the wedding, and it’s best to feel at peace with your decision.

If the shop allows it, take a photo or two. They’ll help you later to spot a detail you’d change or, on the contrary, to feel confident in your choice if any doubts creep in.

And finally: can there be only one? Of course not. If the budget allows, why not enjoy two dresses, one for the first part of the wedding and another for the party? One of our golden rules as wedding planners is: your wedding, your rules. So there you have it: dreaming is allowed.
