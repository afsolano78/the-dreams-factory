Music is with us all our lives. It awakens the senses, stirs memories and, in a heartbeat, takes us back to the moment when one song or another was playing.

That’s why we’re sure your story as a couple is full of songs too: your first dance together, the song that was always on the radio on your first road trip, that concert you arrived late for… All of that is part of your story and, as wedding planners, we’d say that precisely because they have witnessed your story, those songs deserve an invitation to your wedding.

Your wedding is your party and, as at any party, music is essential. When we think about wedding music, we tend to focus only on the party after dinner. Choosing the repertoire and the type of music (DJ, live band…) is key to the success of that moment and therefore of the whole wedding. A good DJ knows how to get guests going by playing one style or another depending on the moment; a band adds an extra touch of class, and combining both can turn the dance floor into a real party with songs for every taste.

Another great moment for live music is the cocktail hour: a jazz band or a string group completes the atmosphere and creates a party feel with real style. In Asturias there’s a wide choice of live bands and DJs for the cocktail hour and the reception.

Music doesn’t have to stop there. As it’s your party, you can fill it with little musical nods that become moments of complicity between you: one of your favourite songs played live as the bride walks in or as you exchange rings, or that track that means so much to you as you enter the reception or leave the ceremony… That said, it’s not about turning the wedding into a music festival: balance is everything.

If you have a wedding planner, she’ll help you organise all those ideas and make them happen. Ask her advice: she knows the best moments for each song, so everything flows naturally.

We’ve saved one small piece of advice for last, for one of the most important musical moments: the first dance. For many couples it’s one of the most emotional moments of the day. If you’re one of them, our advice is that there are no rules. Choose the song that moves you most, the one that meant so much at some point in your life, and dance: dance as if you were alone, looking at each other, enjoying it and singing along to the notes you’ve sung so many times. I promise you’ll remember it forever.

And from that moment on, it will no longer be just a song: it will be the soundtrack of your wedding.
