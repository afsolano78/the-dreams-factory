When we started training as wedding planners, there were barely two wedding planning companies in Asturias, and whenever we introduced ourselves to other businesses or to couples we always heard the same thing: “A wedding what?”. It was unusual; nobody had heard of it except in films. In Madrid and other parts of Spain, however, the profession had already found its place and couples knew it and needed it. We knew that, sooner or later, it would reach Asturias.

And in just a few years, weddings in Asturias have done a complete U-turn. Couples now look for different, special things, full of unique and unforgettable moments, and they care about their guests’ wellbeing at every turn. They want one-of-a-kind weddings, and that’s where we come in.

A wedding planner is, so to speak, an expert wedding adviser. Couples come to us to guide them along the long road to their big day. We are with them every step of the way, always recommending the suppliers that best suit what they need. We share unique moments with them, like choosing the dress, the make-up trial, looking for details, the music, the invitations… We draw up a timeline with every task and its deadline, so that not a single detail slips through and the couple have the peace of mind of knowing they’re doing everything right.

Wedding planners don’t own tablecloths, flowers, chairs or tableware: that’s what furniture-hire and floral professionals are for. We know them and recommend the ones that best fit your idea. That’s our role.

Together with the couple, we design the styling proposals so that every corner has that special something. Some go for a themed wedding, others for something romantic, vintage, rustic, elegant… The options are endless, and we’re there to make sure the wedding is one hundred per cent yours, reflecting your personality, your story and your tastes, and to guide the preparations so that everything makes sense and nothing goes wrong.

Once everything is in place —suppliers, design, décor— the big day arrives. On that day, we’re the ones who keep the nerves! The couple just have to relax and enjoy it. Absolutely everything is written down in our running order, and we oversee every detail: the décor, every corner, the flower arrangements, transport and guests, hair and make-up, musicians, DJ, tables, chairs… And for your wedding planner the big day doesn’t start on the day itself but days before, when many of the preparations you’ve been weaving together for months take place: calls, meetings with suppliers at the venue and those last details couples never have time for.

We always have a plan B: you never know when you’ll need it. And we come prepared with our emergency kit, like Mary Poppins: hairpins, plasters, sun cream!… It’s amazing how you can save a guest’s party with a simple detail. And we’re there for our couples for as long as they need us.

Having a wedding planner makes a difference, not only to the result but to how you live the whole process in the months before. Quite simply, we help you *live* your wedding.
