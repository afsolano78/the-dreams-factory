Don’t deny it: it’s true. From the moment a couple start picturing their wedding day, they begin creating their own little film, like real directors.

There are classic films, romantic ones, alternative ones, big productions and indie films, and couples always know which film they want to be part of. It may sound superficial put like that, but you’ll soon see what I mean.

As in any film, there are stars and supporting actors, and this is where I want to pause. Flowers, colours, lights, tablecloths, cutlery… they are the supporting cast and, as such, they matter: they make the whole thing harmonious, help everything flow and ensure a perfect result. But none of it would make sense without the stars: the couple and their feelings. You.

And, like any good director, your mission is never to lose sight of what matters. In other words: that you, the stars, never lose your script, with all those notes in the margins that took you so many years to write and that hold your story.

The road from the start of the wedding planning is long. Enjoy every step and every decision; when it all gets too much (if it ever does), think about what brought you here, and make the most of every drive to see suppliers to get excited and dream about the day.

Never finalise a preparation or make a decision when you’re in a bad mood or don’t feel like it. If that happens, there will be another time: put it off and swap that plan for a chat for two over coffee and a slice of cake, a walk in your favourite place or a film with popcorn under a blanket.

Beware of advice! We’ll never tire of saying it. It will come with the best intentions, from people who love you and want the best for you, but take it with a pinch of salt and don’t forget it’s your wedding: there are no clichés, no compulsory traditions, no “weird” weddings for wanting to do this or that, and no fear of “that’s just not how it’s done”. If something seems risky or unusual, ask the professionals around you, weigh it up and decide.

We won’t lie to you: turning what’s in your heads into reality, however simple it seems, isn’t that easy. If you feel you can do it and enjoy it, go ahead. If you’re not sure, at The Dreams Factory we’ll walk that road with you and give you the calm the process needs so you can enjoy it.

Creating such an important day in a relaxed, happy atmosphere shows at the wedding. I promise that the memories of the big day will be joined by stories and moments from the months before.

So now you know: don’t let the supporting cast, the extras and the walk-ons steal the leading role. Think about what led you to write that great script and… action!
