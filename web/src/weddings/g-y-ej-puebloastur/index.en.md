How do you bring together two continents, several countries, Asturian tradition and luxury? A mix that seemed complicated at first, but couldn’t have turned out more perfect: an unforgettable wedding.

We planned G and EJ’s dream wedding with them from the other side of the Atlantic. They and their families were the perfect example of what a great party means to us: the couple enjoying their people, the food, the drinks and the dancing, in an incredible atmosphere, surrounded by unique views of Asturias.
